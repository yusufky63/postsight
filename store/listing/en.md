# Store listing (English)

## Name
Postsight: AI fact-check and research for X

## Short description
Fact-check, research, summarize, translate and copy posts on X with your own Claude, OpenAI or DeepSeek API key.

## Description
Postsight adds a small AI button to every post on X. One click gives you:

- Verify: a verdict (accurate, misleading, false, opinion and more), a confidence score, claim-by-claim notes and the sources behind them, using live web search.
- Research: background, key dated facts, who is involved and where people disagree.
- Summarize: a one-line TL;DR and the substance, including threads, quoted posts, X Articles and linked news pages.
- Translate: into your language, with links and mentions kept intact.
- Explain: jargon, references, memes and irony made clear.
- Reply ideas: three drafts in different tones to copy. Postsight never posts for you.
- Ask: any question about the post.
- Copy: the text, the link, or a ready-made quote.

Bring your own key. Postsight works with Claude, OpenAI and DeepSeek. In Auto mode it picks a light, cheap model for simple jobs and a stronger, web-searching model for fact-checks, or you choose a model per post.

Know what you spend. Every result shows tokens, web searches, cost and time. Set monthly budgets per provider and see how much is left and roughly how many tokens that buys.

Reads what posts point to. Postsight reads X Articles and, with your permission, the news and blog pages a post links to, so a link-only post is not judged by its headline.

Wallet cards (optional). Hover a 0x address or a .eth name in a post to see its ENS name or Basename, linked profiles, Ethos reviews, Coinbase verification and GoPlus security flags. For a token the card adds DexScreener price, liquidity and market cap, GeckoTerminal's score and top holders, a Honeypot.is test buy and sell with the real fees, contract checks and who created it. It says when the wallet's names, the token's listed X account or its creator point to the post's author, or to someone else. Public sources, no keys needed, and nothing is sent until you hover. Your own Etherscan or Blockscout PRO key adds contract creators and verified names on more chains.

Private by design. Keys stay in your browser and go only to the provider you chose. There is no Postsight server and no tracking.

Available in English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português, Italiano, Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and Bahasa Indonesia.
